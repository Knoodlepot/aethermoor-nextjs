Aethermoor: The Chronicles

AI-Powered Fantasy Text Adventure

A darkness stirs across the continent. Ancient powers awaken. Nine factions vie for control. The fate of Aethermoor rests with one unlikely soul — perhaps you.

What Is Aethermoor?

A browser-based fantasy RPG powered by Claude AI.

Every response, every story beat, every encounter is generated fresh just for you — no two playthroughs are ever the same.

You don’t read a fixed story. You live one.

Features

⚔ AI-Generated Narrative

Every action you take is answered by a living narrator. Explore, fight, negotiate, deceive — the world reacts to everything you do. Type freely or use the context-sensitive action buttons.

🗺 A Living World to Explore

A procedurally generated continent with capital cities, towns, villages, hamlets, farms, ruins, dungeons, caves, shrines, rivers, and forests — all connected by a full road network. Fast travel between visited locations by foot, horse, barge, or sea vessel.

🧙 Deep Character System

Choose from four classes: Warrior, Rogue, Mage, or Cleric. Level up, earn stat and skill points, and build your hero through a tiered skill tree. Manage HP, gold, rations, reputation, and equipped gear.

⚔ Turn-Based Combat

Attack, use your class ability, defend, dodge, or flee. Enemies have tiers (minion to boss), combat styles, and status effects. Final bosses and villain lieutenants have their own dramatic encounters.

🏰 Nine Factions

Join one of nine factions — from the Warriors’ Guild to the Shadow Court. Earn ranks, unlock faction abilities, collect faction gear sets, and watch your standing with rivals shift. Faction quests and rewards tie into the main story.

📜 Quest System

A full narrative arc unfolds across five acts. Take on side quests, faction quests, and contracts alongside the main story. Track active quests in the sidebar. The Quest Log keeps a full record — completed, active, and failed.

🛒 Shops, Crafting & Economy

Buy and sell gear from merchants whose stock matches the settlement size — hamlets carry basics, capitals stock masterwork and rare items. Craft your own supplies using gathered materials and recipes. Barter directly with merchants through the narrator.

✨ Subclasses

Reach level 10 and choose a subclass to define your playstyle. Twelve subclasses across four classes — Berserker, Knight, or Monk for Warriors; Assassin, Thief, or Ranger for Rogues; Necromancer, Elementalist, or Illusionist for Mages; Paladin, Priest, or Inquisitor for Clerics. Each grants three unique abilities that shape how the narrator responds to you.

🧑 Companions

Recruit a companion to travel alongside you. Companions are living characters — they participate in combat, react to the world, and grow through the story with you. Dismiss them, lose them, or keep them to the end.

🏆 Achievements & New Game+

Thirty achievements to unlock across your adventures. Complete the main quest and carry your legacy into New Game+ — your title, achievements, a keepsake item, and a perk of your choice follow you into the next run, with the world remembering who you were.

🗝 The Dungeon of Echoes

Descend into a procedurally generated dungeon beneath Aethermoor Capital. Floor by floor, face monsters, claim loot, and push deeper — or die trying.

🌐 24 Languages

The AI narrator responds in your chosen language — every story beat, dialogue, and description is generated in the language you select. Core game UI (action buttons, combat menus, quest panels, and context labels) also translates. Note: secondary screens such as character creation, inventory, and settings remain in English.

Supported: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Lithuanian, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, Turkish, Greek, Welsh, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Brazilian Portuguese, and Thai.

Plus six fun narrator modes: Latin, Pirate, Old Norse, Shakespearean, Klingon, and Orc.

♿ Accessibility

Four colorblind-safe colour themes, optional dyslexia-friendly font, and three text sizes — all settable from the main menu Options screen.

Play In Your Browser

No download. No install. Works on desktop and mobile. The mobile layout is fully optimised — narrative fills the screen, action buttons appear below the input bar, and the full character panel, map, and quests are a tap away. Just click Play and your legend begins.
